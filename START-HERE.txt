Sarah Maria — Mermaid Adventure
v0.8.0 — Boss Polish / Opal Current

1. Extract the whole ZIP into a NEW EMPTY folder.
2. Import the ROOT project.godot in Godot 4.4 or newer. Tested with 4.4.1.
3. Keep godot/ and dist/ beside project.godot. Wait for asset import.
4. Press F5. Begin adventure starts the family lagoon tutorial.
   Choose a chapter opens any of the eight chapters.
   Continue adventure restores your last checkpoint, unlocks and Avina choice.
   Checkpoints and chapter results save automatically; stars and best scores stay.

Swim: arrows / WASD / controller stick / touch pad
Boost: Space / controller B
Jump: J or Shift / controller A
Bubble: hold B or Z / controller X / touch Bubble, then RELEASE.
Quick release: Pearl Pop. Hold 0.35s: Prism Bloom. Hold 1.35s: Royal Aurora.
Mint petals + star: valid hit. Amber shield: guarded. Lavender: hit cooldown.
The charge meter shows your tier. COOLING means wait for the next bubble.
The tutorial’s super-bubble practice target is optional; its portal stays open.
Pause: Escape / P / controller Start

Use landscape for play. Portrait pauses safely.
Options includes audio, Gentle Motion, Economy graphics and touch controls.

Princess Polish Sarah adds finer coordinated motion, a torpedo boost pose,
and layered swimming wakes. Gentle Motion and Economy suppress extra effects.

This is the cleaned playable project. Tests, capture scripts and historical
reports remain in the GitHub repository rather than the playable ZIP.

Bubble tuning: Pearl Pop 1 power / 0.20s recovery; Prism Bloom 2 / 0.35s;
Royal Aurora 4 / 0.45s against ordinary enemies. Boss damage stays capped at 2.
Charged bubbles travel faster; guard and vulnerability rules still apply.

Boss tips: leave marked attack lanes, then attack during mint recovery.
Lavender arcs mean the last hit is cooling. Carlo teaches Boost before Bubble.
Duke and Flip share a comedy opening; Rana clears her pearls before her laser.
