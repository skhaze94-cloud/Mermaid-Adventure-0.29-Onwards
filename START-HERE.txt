v0.28: the comprehensive enemy animation update.
Each crab, eel, jellyfish, pufferfish, swordfish and shark now has 16 actual
joint-motion clips, up from four portrait poses. Each clip has 96 intervals
of sampled motion with seamless interpolation and smooth clip blending.
A persistent 1,617-vertex skin bends the original painted creature itself:
- Crab: both claw wrists, opposed pincer jaws and alternating walking legs.
- Eel: articulated swimming spine and delayed tail flexion.
- Jellyfish: bell contraction and independently lagging tentacle strands.
- Pufferfish: inflation, tail kick and fin paddling.
- Swordfish / shark: two tail hinges, pectoral paddle, dorsal fin and banking.
Carlo, Duke Claw, Miguel, Flip, Baron Bite, Rana Rex, Nessie and the palace
performance use the same 16-clip system with species-specific articulation.
Combat colors remain immediate. Animation never moves the collision root.
Paused clocks stay still; Gentle Motion and Economy settings remain supported.

The complete v0.27 character update, family tutorial, Avina choices, chapter
menus, transitions and all eight levels are included.
WASD/arrows: move. Space: Boost. J/Shift: Jump. B/Z: Bubble.
P/Escape: pause. R: restart. Touch and controller controls included.

Validated with Linux Godot 4.3: 24 automated suites, native chapter renders,
16-second native animation preview, and fresh ZIP extraction/import.
