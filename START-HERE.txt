Sarah Maria — Mermaid Adventure | Godot v0.29.5 — Fluid Torso

Extract into a NEW EMPTY folder. Import the ROOT project.godot in Godot 4.3+.
Keep godot/ and dist/ beside project.godot. Wait for asset import, then press F5.
Begin adventure starts the family tutorial. Choose a chapter opens any of the
eight levels directly.

v0.29.5 adds independent ribcage rotation/lift/volume, delayed waist-to-tail
flow, and a neck/collar that follows the chest. See godot/SARAH-TORSO-REVIEW.md.
The v0.29.4 Definitive Sarah review and 432-point motion libraries are retained.

v0.29.2 adds a gentle back ripple, chest-led turns and delayed neck settling.
The collar and hip joins remain anchored. See godot/SARAH-SPINE-REVIEW.md.

v0.29.1 adds subtle blinks, gaze, smiles, ear/nose head-follow and hair motion
coordinated with the body, tail, strokes, boosts and waterfall current.
See godot/SARAH-DETAILS-VALIDATION.md for the detail polish checks.

v0.29 focuses on Sarah: 36 body/fluke loops, 432 samples per loop, twelve
distinct swim-stroke profiles and six aerial performances. Swim horizontally,
up, down or boost to see different arm strokes and matching tail movement.
Continued swimming naturally alternates related strokes. The waterfall has
dedicated entry, balance/sculling, climb, dive, boost and exit performances.
Stroke changes blend smoothly without resetting the animation phase.

WASD/arrows: move. Space: Boost. J/Shift: Jump. B/Z: Bubble.
P/Escape: pause. R: restart. Touch and controller controls are included.
Gentle Motion and Economy settings remain available.

See godot/ULTIMATE-SARAH-VALIDATION.md for this release's checks and limits.
