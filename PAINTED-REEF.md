# v0.22.0 — The Painted Reef

A focused scenery finish over the complete v0.21 project. Cavern platforms now have a dark recessed outer seam, a softly rounded inner bevel, a readable illuminated lip and three shallow mineral inclusions with individual placement. These details remain inside the original platform silhouette. Treasure supports receive a small polished edge rather than floating below the chest.

Across the other chapters, painted rock barriers now use the existing cached deformable rock skin previously used only in the tutorial. The underlying painted contours remain transparent, with modest individual silhouette and colour variation. Highland relief sprites also receive restrained individual tint and facing variation. Collision rectangles, routes, treasure values, input, dialogue, progression, saves, characters and all existing paintings remain unchanged.

This is a refinement of the established painted materials, not a replacement of every item with new artwork. Shared stone materials and collectible symbols remain. The v0.21 reports and measurements retained in this project describe that prior release; they are not new v0.22 performance measurements. Physical Android device testing remains outstanding.

Validation: 31 clean-package suites and import/startup pass; four final APK resource checks pass. The certificate and app ID are retained, with version 0.22.0 / 2200. Matching-size visual comparison and raw software desktop samples are included in verification/painted-reef/. These samples are diagnostic, not Android frame-rate measurements.
