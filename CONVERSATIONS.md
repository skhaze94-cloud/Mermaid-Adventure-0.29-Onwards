# A Little Room to Talk — v0.15.0

The tutorial teaches one action at a time. Only the family introduction, gifts, trial and congratulations automatically pause for a conversation. Other lessons keep flowing; Help reopens the appropriate conversation. Use H on a keyboard, or tap Help. Reading pauses hazards and swimming.

Continue advances a single speaker’s beat, then returns to the water. Say something opens Sarah’s three optional replies; selecting one plays the matching response and remembers its tone for the farewell. Little extras contains the optional Daddy costume and a conversation with Mum when available. Back closes optional choices first, then returns to the lesson. These actions never award repeated rewards or change training requirements.

The final Bubble help explains horizontal aiming at the same depth, the 1.35-second Royal Aurora hold, and the difference between Sarah’s and Avina’s bubbles. Practising on the target is optional; the portal still works without it.

The companion decision has two equally valid choices. Its send-off uses the same Continue action; it never shows lesson shortcuts or unrelated replies. Existing companion ownership and onward progression remain intact.

There is one tutorial lesson cue, no persistent score/chapter stack, no row of three teacher buttons and no duplicate trial bar. Outside the tutorial, the small score shell, hearts and aqua energy remain readable. Charge feedback appears only while relevant. Neighbour captions give way to gameplay speech, and longer messages wrap with reading time based on their length.

Speech uses a short fade, with immediate text under Gentle Motion. Controller focus follows each new action. The primary action stays below the scrolling content; wide and small landscape layouts show all three replies. No dialogue advances on a reading timer.
