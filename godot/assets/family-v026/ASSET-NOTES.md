# Family art provenance
Original king-daddy-atlas.webp, queen-antonella-atlas.webp, tutorial-magic-atlas.webp, tutorial-reef-v6.webp and tutorial-daddy-faces-v6.webp were decoded from the user-supplied 8.2 HTML offline asset map. Original body/portrait atlas structure is retained for reference; expression portraits are consumed by the dialogue UI.

king-body-v026.webp and queen-body-v026.webp are new generated transparent character bodies referenced to those original atlases. Avina’s avina-baby-v026.png is generated from the approved baby character concept. These are raster assets; their connected mesh motion is authored in GDScript. No anatomical asset splitting or duplicated joints is used.
