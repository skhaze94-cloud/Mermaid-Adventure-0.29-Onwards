# Level 1 creature art — v0.9

Six new alpha sprite atlases were generated for this update; the gentleman shark directly reuses the user-provided 05-61029.png sheet. Existing game art and supplied references guided the character identities. WebP conversion preserves alpha; source PNGs are retained outside the repository. Pose order: patrol top-left, windup top-right, attack bottom-left, recovery bottom-right. Frame rectangles are explicitly adjusted for broad claws; the supplied shark uses measured bounds because its poses are not spaced on an exact grid. Shark uses four selected right-facing cells from its 4×3 source sheet. Godot fits cells uniformly, without stretching anatomy.

The models are 2D painted sprites, not 3D meshes. Contact geometry, encounter count and attack timing remain as before; two existing swordfish patrols now present as gentleman sharks with the same lunge behaviour. Combat poses switch immediately; decorative detail updates are capped at 30 Hz (20 Hz in Economy), with visibility culling and pooled resources. Gentle motion retains essential combat pose changes while removing bobbing, body deformation and defeat fades.
