# Sarah title artwork — Godot v0.8

`sarah-title-v08.webp` is dedicated title-screen key art created with the built-in image generation tool. Its reference is the repository's `dist/assets/sarah-mermaid.webp`. The reference is used to preserve Sarah's identity and clothing; the new illustration has an upright swimming pose, a glowing pearl, and a genuine alpha background. The output is 1024 × 1536 RGBA, exported to WebP at quality 92. Its alpha is preserved. It is used as an independent Godot TextureRect over the existing Highland painting, never as a flattened menu screenshot.

The title scenery remains the existing `dist/assets/highlands.webp`. Music remains `dist/assets/bubble-bell-adventure.mp3`. Font attribution and licences remain in `dist/fonts/`.
